# Image generation prompts

Use original imagery. Do not generate fake hospital logos, fake clinician endorsements, identifiable patient records, real medical charts, or text-heavy UI screenshots with gibberish.

“Authentic editorial portrait of a small diverse health technology product team collaborating in a bright office, natural expressions, professional but approachable, no lab coats unless contextually accurate, no logos, no text, wide 4:3.”

## Asset rules
- Prefer buyer-supplied licensed assets for real product screenshots.
- Add meaningful alt text; mark decorative images appropriately.
- Optimize file size and use suitable responsive crops.
- Do not present generated imagery as evidence of real clinical use.
