# Context and product positioning

## Working brand
**Clinica AI** — a fictional brand used to demonstrate a credible healthcare AI company website. The buyer can rename and rebrand it.

## Audience
- Founders and product marketers at healthcare AI startups
- Clinical workflow and documentation software companies
- Medical imaging and diagnostics AI vendors
- Healthcare data and analytics platforms
- Clinical research technology providers

## Buyer problem
Generic AI SaaS templates often communicate features but do not provide enough healthcare-specific information architecture for trust-sensitive buyers. This template should help a company explain its intended clinical workflow, human oversight, data handling, deployment model and evidence without implying clinical validation that does not exist.

## Positioning
A calm, evidence-aware, enterprise-ready website system for healthcare AI products. It should feel modern and technically capable without looking like a hospital brochure or making unsupported medical claims.

## Primary conversion
Request a product demonstration.

## Secondary conversions
Explore platform capabilities, read research/resources, contact sales and subscribe to product updates (only if a real form integration is configured).

## Messaging principles
1. Explain the workflow before claiming benefits.
2. Distinguish assistive software from clinical decision-making.
3. Explain where qualified professionals remain in control.
4. Use explicit placeholders for compliance and certifications until buyer-supplied evidence is provided.
5. Never invent statistics, clinical outcomes, customer logos, testimonials, regulatory approvals or partnerships.

## Brand voice
Clear, measured, human, technically literate and reassuring. Avoid hype such as “revolutionary”, “diagnoses everything”, “100% accurate” or “replaces clinicians”.

## Content disclaimer
Clinica AI is fictional. All product screens and workflows are illustrative and are not medical advice, a validated medical device, or evidence of regulatory clearance.
